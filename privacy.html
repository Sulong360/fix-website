Data Privacy Notice

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) · Last updated: October 2026

FIX Philippines ("FIX", "we") connects homeowners with verified contractors. This notice explains what personal information we collect through our website and client portal, why we collect it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, mobile number and email address
  • Project details: project type, home type, location, lot size, budget and notes
  • Account and activity data: login records, offers received, project status and, when available, messages and documents you upload
  • Basic technical data such as browser and device information

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account and send your registration and login links
  • To review your project and match you with verified contractors
  • To let you track offers and project progress
  • To contact you about your request and improve our service
  • To comply with legal obligations

We process your information based on your consent and to provide the service you requested.

3. Who we share it with

Your project details, and your contact details once matched, are shared only with the contractors matched to your project. We use service providers to run the platform, including cloud hosting, database and email delivery providers, who may store data outside the Philippines. They may only use it to provide their services to us. We do not sell your personal information.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed for the purposes above or required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

5. How we protect it

We use access controls, role-based permissions and encrypted connections. Contractors can only see a project after FIX has reviewed it.

6. Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object, to correct it, to request erasure or blocking, to data portability, to claim damages where applicable, and to withdraw your consent at any time. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

7. Contact us

To exercise your rights or ask a question, message us on WhatsApp at +63 992 812 2086 or through our Facebook page.

8. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
