Why go solar

The smartest home upgrade you can make in the Philippines

Electricity rates in the Philippines are among the highest in Asia. Solar pays for itself — and then keeps paying you.

💡
Cut your Meralco bill by up to 80%
Philippine electricity rates average ₱10–12/kWh and keep rising. A properly sized solar system can eliminate most of your monthly bill from day one.
🌱
25-year panel lifespan
Modern solar panels are built to last decades. Most come with a 25-year performance warranty — meaning guaranteed savings long after the system pays itself off.
🏠
Increases your property value
Homes with solar systems sell faster and at higher prices. It's an infrastructure upgrade that adds lasting value to your property.
🔋
Battery storage options available
Add a battery backup to store excess solar energy for nighttime use or brownouts — full energy independence for your home.
✅
FIX-verified installers only

📋
